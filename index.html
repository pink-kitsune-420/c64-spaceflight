C64 Spaceflight

An orbital flight sim written in 6502 assembly for the Commodore 64, running here on a small JavaScript C64. Burn to climb, coast to orbit, warp time to watch the orbit come round.

Joystick
Keys

On a keyboard: ← → turn, Space or ↑ burns, and the keys above work as typed (+ − zoom too). On a phone, hold the keycaps.

Reading the HUD

ALT
Height above the surface, in world units
AP / PE
Highest and lowest point of the predicted orbit (the yellow crosses)
ZOOM
0 is closest, 7 furthest
W
Time warp, ×1 to ×256 (held back near the ground, while burning, or when moving fast)
VX VY
Velocity as 8.8 hex: whole part, then fraction; FF.. is just below zero
HDG
Heading in hex, 256 steps a turn: 00 up, 40 right, 80 down, C0 left
CAM
SHIP follows you, PLNT centres the planet

How it flies

Physics runs 50 times a second in the raster interrupt, in fixed point: 16.16 positions, 8.24 velocities, and gravity of exactly −GM/r³ · position from an exact r². The dotted line is a leapfrog predictor flying a ghost copy of the ship ahead, one orbit at a time.

Press P to see the cost: the border turns red while the physics runs.

Planet radius 512 u · surface gravity 0.02 u/frame² · engine 0.05 u/frame² · warp up to 256 frames per step

This is the real program, spaceflight.prg, running instruction by instruction on an emulated 6502 at 50 frames a second. The emulator was written for this page: no Commodore ROMs are included, and the lettering is an original stand-in for the C64 character set.
